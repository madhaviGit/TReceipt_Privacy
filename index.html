Privacy Policy for TReceipts

Last updated: October 8, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TReceipts handles data when you use the app. TReceipts minimizes data collection, processes all data locally on your device, and complies with applicable data protection laws, including GDPR in the EU.

By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Receipt images are processed locally and are not stored permanently or transmitted to external servers.

Data We May Process

Receipt Data

When you upload or capture receipt images, the app extracts relevant purchase information (e.g., store name, purchase date, total amount). This processing is done entirely on your device and the images are deleted after extraction.

Usage Data

The app may collect limited, anonymous diagnostic data such as device type, operating system version, and general crash information to improve app performance. This data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify you.

Crash Reporting

Crash reports are collected using Firebase Crashlytics only if you consent. Crash data includes:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App state at the time of the crash
  • Technical details such as stack traces

No personal information is included in crash reports. You can opt out of crash reporting in the app settings at any time.

App Permissions

  • Camera: To allow you to take photos of receipts within the app.
  • Photo Library / Media Access: To upload existing receipt images.
  • Storage / App-Specific Storage: Excel files are saved in app-specific directories. No special storage permission is required and files are not shared without your action.

File Storage and Export

Excel export files are saved in a dedicated app-specific folder on your device. Files are not shared outside this folder, and no external storage permission is required. You can access the exported files via your device's file manager.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided personal data, please contact us to remove it.

Third-Party Services

We use Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting. No other third-party services collect personal data. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

No personal data is collected or stored on your device. Extracted receipt data (such as total amount, seller name, and date of purchase) are saved in Firebase under an anonymous account to allow app functionality and data synchronization. This data is not linked to your identity and is retained only as necessary for the app’s features.

Data Security

All processing occurs locally on your device. No personal data is transmitted externally, so there is minimal risk of unauthorized access.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted in the app. Please review periodically.
