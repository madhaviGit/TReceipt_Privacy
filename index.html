Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected Personal Data

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

Any personal information that might be visible on uploaded receipt images (e.g., store name, card type) is used solely for extracting purchase-related data. This information is not linked to your identity and is not shared or stored beyond the scope of the app’s intended features.

Usage Data

We may collect limited, anonymized usage data, such as device type, OS version, and general diagnostic information, to help maintain and improve the Service. This data is non-identifiable and cannot be used to trace any individual user.

Information Collected while Using the Application

While using Our Application, in order to provide features of Our Application, We may collect, with Your prior permission: - Images of receipts and related documents from your Device's camera or photo library We use this information to extract relevant data such as the store name, purchase date, total amount, and other receipt-specific details. **TReceipt does not store receipt images locally or remotely.** All image processing occurs on your device, and once data is extracted, the images are discarded. We do not use these images for any purpose unrelated to the core functionality of the Application. You can enable or disable access to this information at any time through Your Device settings.

Permissions Required

Our Application may request access to the following device permissions to function properly: - Camera: To allow you to take photos of receipts within the app - Photo Library/Media Access: To upload existing receipt images - Storage: To store and process images temporarily (if applicable) You may grant or revoke these permissions at any time via your device’s system settings.
