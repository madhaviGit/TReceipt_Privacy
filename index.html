TReceipts does not collect personal identifiers such as names, email addresses, or contact details. The app may access receipt images provided by You and may collect non-identifiable usage or device data to ensure proper functionality and improve the Service.

By using the Service, You agree to the handling of data in accordance with this Privacy Policy. TReceipts processes data locally on your device and complies with GDPR and other applicable data protection laws by minimizing data collection and preventing transmission of personal data to external servers.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Personal Data

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

Any personal information visible on uploaded receipt images (e.g., store name, card type) is used solely for extracting purchase-related data. This information is not linked to your identity and is not shared or stored beyond the scope of the app’s intended features.

Usage Data

We may collect limited, anonymized usage data such as device type, OS version, and general diagnostic information to help maintain and improve the Service. This data is non-identifiable and cannot be used to trace any individual user.

Information Collected while Using the Application

While using Our Application, in order to provide its features, We may collect, with Your prior permission:

  • Images of receipts and related documents from your Device's camera or photo library

We use this information to extract relevant data such as the store name, purchase date, total amount, and other receipt-specific details.

TReceipts does not store receipt images locally or remotely. All image processing occurs on your device, and once data is extracted, the images are discarded. We do not use these images for any purpose unrelated to the core functionality of the Application. You can enable or disable access to this information at any time through your Device settings.

Permissions Required

  • Camera: To allow you to take photos of receipts within the app
  • Photo Library/Media Access: To upload existing receipt images
  • Storage: To temporarily process receipt images and create export files (CSV/Excel). No receipt images or files are permanently stored or shared.

Use of Your Personal Data

We do not collect or use any personal data. The Application functions entirely on your device and does not require you to provide any personally identifiable information.

Retention of Your Personal Data

We do not collect or store any personal data, and therefore no data is retained.

Transfer of Your Personal Data

We do not collect or store any personal data; therefore, no personal data is transferred to any third-party servers or outside your country or region.

Delete Your Personal Data

As we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal information to delete. Receipt images are processed on your device and not stored locally or remotely.

Disclosure of Your Personal Data

We do not collect any personal data and therefore have no information to disclose to any third parties or legal authorities.

Security of Your Personal Data

We do not collect or store any personal data. All receipt images are processed locally on your device and are not saved permanently or transmitted to any servers. Since no personal data is collected, there is no risk of exposure or unauthorized access to user data.

Children's Privacy

If You are a parent or guardian and become aware that Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us. If We learn that We have collected Personal Data from anyone under the age of 13 without parental consent, We will remove that information promptly.

No personal data is stored or transmitted. Data processing is carried out solely based on your explicit consent in accordance with Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR. You can disable crash data collection at any time in the app settings or withdraw your consent.

For more information, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy.
